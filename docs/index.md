# Levoit Purifier for Indigo

This plugin brings Levoit air purifiers into [Indigo](https://www.indigodomo.com). It signs in to your VeSync account, the same one the Levoit app uses, and shows each purifier as an Indigo device you can switch on and off, with its PM2.5, air quality, fan speed, mode and filter life, and actions for everything else the app can set.

Everything goes through VeSync's own servers, the same way the Levoit app does, so the Mac that runs Indigo needs to reach the internet and each purifier needs to be on Wi-Fi and showing in the app.

It was written for the Core 400S.

## What it does for you

- **Shows each purifier in Indigo** — on or off, how clean the air is, the fan speed, the mode and how much life the filter has left.
- **Switches it on and off** like any other Indigo device, so schedules, triggers, control pages and Dashboards can all use it.
- **Sets the fan speed, mode, child lock, display and a switch-off timer**, and resets the filter life after you fit a new filter.
- **Reads the purifier back after every command**, so the device shows what the purifier is doing, not what was asked for.
- **Tells you when a purifier drops off VeSync** and when its filter is nearly worn out.
